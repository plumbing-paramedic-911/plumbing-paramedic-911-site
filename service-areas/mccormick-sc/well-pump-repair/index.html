McCormick well problems diagnosed before parts are replaced

A no-water call does not automatically mean the pump is dead. We test the breaker and controls, pressure switch, tank air charge, wiring and system pressure before recommending a repair. That separates a smaller electrical or tank problem from a pump that actually needs to be pulled.

Well pump and pressure services

  • Submersible and jet pump diagnosis, repair and replacement
  • Deep-well service, including systems around 360 feet
  • Pressure tank, pressure switch and control-box replacement
  • Low-pressure, short-cycling and constantly running pump diagnosis
  • Well-water sediment, iron and sulfur troubleshooting
  • Emergency no-water dispatch for McCormick County

What affects your final price

Well depth and pump horsepower are only part of the job. Access to the well head, pipe and wire condition, failed check valves, damaged fittings and the size of the pressure tank can change the scope. We inspect first and put the exact price in writing before work begins.
