Internal Dispatch SMS Consent

Plumbing Paramedic 911, LLC · Owner and authorized on-call personnel

Published October 24, 2026

This page documents the enrollment procedure and disclosures for Plumbing Paramedic 911’s internal dispatch text alerts. It is not a public customer signup form. Publishing or visiting this page does not subscribe anyone, activate messaging, or establish that a particular recipient has consented.

What these alerts are for

Darla, our automated receptionist, is being configured to notify the owner or an authorized on-call plumber when an urgent plumbing caller cannot be transferred. Alerts may contain a caller’s name, callback number, service location, problem summary and transfer outcome. This program is for internal service coordination, not advertising or customer marketing.

Program status: Setup and verification are in progress. Automated alerts must not be activated until recipient consent, sender approval, opt-out handling and delivery tests are complete.

How opt-in is collected

  1. The owner or designated administrator provides the disclosure below directly to the authorized on-call recipient before enrollment.
  2. The recipient voluntarily confirms consent in writing, or verbally to the administrator. The recipient must identify the mobile number they control; permission is not assumed from employment, being on call, or a customer service request.
  3. The administrator retains a private consent record containing the recipient’s name, mobile number, date, method, disclosure provided, and affirmative response. Verbal consent is documented in a dated written record.
  4. Only the confirmed recipient is added to the dispatch notification destination. Consent records and private mobile numbers are not published on this page.

Disclosure provided before enrollment

Do you agree to receive internal Plumbing Paramedic 911 dispatch alerts at the mobile number you provide, including notifications when Darla cannot transfer an urgent caller? Message frequency varies with service calls; the initial program is estimated at up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing plumbing services. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You can also request enrollment or removal by calling 864-446-8911 or emailing plumbingparamedic911@gmail.com. Review the SMS terms and privacy information on this page.

Example affirmative response: “Yes, I agree to receive Plumbing Paramedic 911 internal dispatch alerts at the mobile number I provide, and I understand the message frequency, possible charges and opt-out instructions.”

This example describes the consent process; it is not an individual’s consent record. The actual recipient must provide their own affirmative response, which is retained privately.

SMS program terms

  • Program: Plumbing Paramedic 911 Internal Dispatch Alerts.
  • Audience: The owner and individually authorized on-call personnel who opt in.
  • Frequency: Varies with urgent calls and failed transfers; estimated up to 100 messages per month for the initial program.
  • Charges: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP to stop SMS alerts. Do not resume alerts without renewed consent. You may also request removal through the office.
  • Help: Reply HELP, call 864-446-8911, or email plumbingparamedic911@gmail.com.
  • Consent applies only to this internal alert program. It does not authorize marketing messages.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. SMS delivery is not guaranteed; alerts do not confirm that a plumber has accepted or been dispatched.
  • Internal dispatch texts are not a substitute for 911 or another emergency service.

SMS privacy information

We use recipient mobile numbers, consent records and dispatch information to operate this internal alert program, honor opt-outs and troubleshoot delivery. We do not sell or share mobile information, SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Messaging and hosting providers, including Twilio, may process necessary information solely to deliver and support these service alerts. Access to caller information and consent records is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers supporting the program.

For access, correction or removal requests, contact the office. See also our Privacy Policy and general Terms of Service.

Example dispatch alert

Plumbing Paramedic 911: URGENT — Darla could not reach the on-call plumber. Caller: [name]. Callback: [number]. Location: [service location]. Issue: [problem]. Please review intake and call back. Reply STOP to opt out; HELP for help.

All bracketed values above are placeholders, not customer information.
