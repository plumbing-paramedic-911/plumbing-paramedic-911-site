About This Service

Tankless Water Heater Installation & Repair

Why go tankless?

A traditional tank heater keeps 40 to 80 gallons of water hot around the clock — even at 3 AM when nobody's using it. A tankless unit fires only when you open a hot-water tap, heats the water on demand, and shuts off when you close the tap. The result: you never run out of hot water, and your energy bill for water heating drops 20 to 40 percent depending on the model and your usage.

Tankless units also last longer — 20 years is typical with annual maintenance, versus 8 to 12 for a traditional tank. They take up far less space (wall-mounted, about the size of a carry-on suitcase), and they eliminate the risk of a 50-gallon tank rupture flooding your utility room.

What we handle

  • New tankless water heater installation — gas or electric, whole-house or point-of-use
  • Tankless repair — no hot water, error codes, flow sensor issues, ignition failure, temperature fluctuation
  • Annual descaling and flushing — removes mineral buildup and keeps your unit running at peak efficiency, especially important in Greenwood and other hard-water areas
  • Tank-to-tankless conversion — replacing an old tank-style heater with a tankless system, including any gas line, venting, or electrical upgrades needed

Where we install

Whether you're building new, replacing a failed tank heater, or upgrading for efficiency, we'll walk you through gas vs. electric, sizing for your household, and what the install involves — before you commit to anything.
